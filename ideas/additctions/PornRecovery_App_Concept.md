# Christian Porn Recovery App — Initial Concept

**Date:** 28 March 2026  
**Status:** Early Idea / Brain Dump

---

## The Problem

Christians struggling with sexual addiction, particularly pornography, often face:
- Shame and fear of exposure within their church community
- Reluctance to seek help from people who know them personally
- Lack of safe, anonymous spaces for accountability and recovery
- Distrust of secular therapeutic approaches

---

## Core Concept

A **faith-based, anonymous porn recovery app** that connects struggling Christians with Spirit-led counsellors (prophets/pastors) in a fully anonymous environment.

---

## Key Principles

- **Full anonymity on both sides** — users and counsellors never know each other's real identity; all interaction is mediated exclusively through the app
- **Faith-centred approach** — counsellors are prophets, pastors, or spiritually mature believers
- **Voluntary giving, not compulsion** — users may reward/appreciate their counsellor anonymously, but it is never mandatory
- **Safe space** — no judgment, no exposure, no risk of community embarrassment

---

## How It Might Work (Rough Flow)

1. User signs up anonymously (no real name, no identifiable info required)
2. User is matched with an available prophet/pastor counsellor
3. All sessions, messages, or calls happen within the app — never directly
4. User can optionally send an anonymous gift/donation to their counsellor as an act of appreciation
5. Counsellor sees no user identity — only a session ID or anonymous alias

## Open Questions / Things to Think Through

- How is the anonymous payment/gifting handled technically and legally?
- Moderation: how to handle fake or predatory counsellors?
- Streak/progress tracking for accountability — anonymous to counsellor?
- Community element: anonymous peer support groups?

3. **No compulsory payment** — giving is voluntary and anonymous
