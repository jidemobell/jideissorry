## Why NOT Use Personal Account for Dating App

### Legal Risks
❌ **Personal Liability:** Dating apps have high legal risk (content moderation, data privacy, user safety). If sued, your personal assets are exposed.

❌ **Tax Complexity:** Mixing commercial income with personal account creates messy tax returns. Irish Revenue might audit personal accounts receiving business income.

### Business Risks
❌ **No Exit Strategy:** If dating app succeeds and you want to sell company or raise investment, having it under personal name kills the deal.

❌ **Brand Confusion:** App shows YOUR PERSONAL NAME as developer. Users see "Jidemobell Okunola" (or similar) instead of "Atensai."

❌ **Transfer Nightmare:** Moving app from personal to company account later requires:
- Preserving ratings/reviews (difficult)
- User notification (bad UX)
- Possible app downtime
- Apple approval (not guaranteed)
- Legal transfer agreement

### Professional Image
❌ **Not Scalable:** Personal accounts look unprofessional for commercial products. Investors, partners, and users expect registered companies.

## FAQs

**Q: Can I transfer apps from personal to company account later?**  
A: Yes, but it's complex. You use "App Transfer" feature in App Store Connect. You lose ratings/reviews, confuse users, and need legal agreements. Better to start commercial apps on company account from day one.

**Q: Will having two Apple Developer accounts cause issues?**  
A: No, it's common. Many developers have personal + company accounts. Just use different emails (personal Gmail vs admin@atensai.com).

**Q: Do I need to register company in Nigeria or Ireland?**  
A: Ireland is better since you live there. Irish company = EU market access, easier banking, simpler taxes in Ireland. Can expand to Nigeria later if needed.

**Q: What if my company registration is rejected?**  
A: Rare if you follow CRO rules. Worst case, choose different company name and resubmit (no extra fee if rejected within 28 days).

**Q: Can I use Revolut personal instead of business bank account?**  
A: Not recommended. Mixing personal/business finances creates tax nightmares and looks unprofessional. Business account is only €0/month with Revolut Business.

**Q: What about Play Store (Google)?**  
A: Similar logic applies. Personal Google Play Console account costs $25 one-time (cheap). But for commercial dating app, register company Play Console (also $25). Same hybrid strategy works.

**Q: Should I get lawyer for company registration?**  
A: Not necessary for simple private limited company. Use online service like [Companies.ie](https://companies.ie) or do it yourself on CRO website. Only need lawyer if complex ownership structure.

**Q: What if I can't afford €99 + €100 + accountant fees right now?**  
A: Start with ONLY personal Apple Developer (€99). Test finance app. Save up for company registration. Launch dating app in 3-6 months when company is ready. Don't rush commercial launch without proper structure.
