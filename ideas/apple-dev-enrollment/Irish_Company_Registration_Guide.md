# Irish Company Registration Guide (For Non-Irish Nationals)

## Overview for Nigerian Nationals Living in Ireland

You can register an Irish company even if you're not an Irish citizen. The process is the same, but there are a few considerations.

---

## Eligibility

### ✅ You CAN Register Irish Company If:
- You're legally living in Ireland (visa, work permit, or residency)
- You have Irish address (residential or business)
- You're over 18 years old
- You have no criminal record involving fraud/dishonesty

### Your Situation:
✅ Nigerian national living in Ireland → **Fully eligible**  
✅ Have Irish address → **Required for company registration**  
✅ No additional restrictions as non-EU national living in Ireland legally

---

## Legal Status After Company Registration

### What You Get:
- **Irish private limited company** (Ltd or Limited)
- **Irish company registration number** (CRO number)
- **Irish tax number** (from Revenue)
- **EU market access** (company is Irish, benefits from EU Single Market)
- **Limited liability protection** (company debts separate from personal)

### What You DON'T Need:
- ❌ Irish citizenship (not required)
- ❌ EU passport (not required)
- ❌ Business visa (not required if you already have right to live/work in Ireland)
- ❌ Minimum capital (€1 share capital is sufficient)

### Important Note:
**Your company is Irish** (not Nigerian), even though you're Nigerian national. The company's nationality is determined by where it's registered, not director's nationality.

## Company Registration Requirements

### 1. Company Name
**Rules:**
- Must end with "Limited" or "Ltd"
- Must be unique (check CRO database first)
- Cannot be offensive or misleading
- Cannot include "Bank", "Insurance", "University" without permission

**Good Options for You:**
- "Atensai Limited" (if available)
- "Atensai Technology Limited"
- "Atensai Digital Limited"
- "Atensai Solutions Limited"

**Check Availability:**
- Go to [core.cro.ie](https://core.cro.ie)
- Search your preferred name
- If nothing shows up → likely available
- Reserve name during registration process

---

### 2. Director Requirements

**Minimum:**
- At least 1 director required
- At least 1 director must be EEA resident (EU + Iceland, Norway, Liechtenstein)

**✅ You're Covered:**
Since you live in Ireland (EEA country), you qualify as EEA resident director.

**If you weren't EEA resident:**
You'd need to either:
- Appoint Irish resident co-director, OR
- Post €25,000 bond with Irish Revenue (not required in your case)

**Your Setup:**
- Single director (you) → ✅ Allowed
- EEA resident (Ireland) → ✅ Requirement met

---

### 3. Registered Office Address

**Required:**
- Physical address in Republic of Ireland (not Northern Ireland)
- Cannot be PO Box
- Must be accessible during business hours
- Will be public record (CRO shows it)

**Options:**

#### Option A: Your Home Address
- ✅ Cheapest (€0)
- ⚠️ Your address becomes public (anyone can look it up on CRO)
- ⚠️ Company mail comes to your home

#### Option B: Registered Office Service
- ✅ Privacy protected (service address shown, not yours)
- ✅ Professional image
- ✅ Mail forwarding included
- Cost: €150-300/year
- Providers: Duffy & Associates, Formations Ireland, Companies.ie

#### Option C: Co-working Space
- ✅ Professional business address
- ✅ May include desk/meeting room access
- Cost: €200-500/month (expensive for early stage)

**Recommendation for You:**
Start with **your home address** (Option A) to save money. You can change registered office address later (simple form, €50 fee) once company generates revenue.

**Privacy Note:**
CRO shows director name + registered office address publicly. If privacy is major concern, use registered office service (Option B).

---

### 4. Company Constitution

**What it is:**
Legal document defining how company operates (like bylaws).

**Options:**
- **Model Constitution:** CRO provides standard template (recommended for simple companies)
- **Custom Constitution:** Lawyer drafts custom rules (only if special requirements)

**For You:**
Use **Model Constitution** from CRO (standard, free, sufficient for 99% of small companies).

---

### 5. Shares and Shareholders

**Minimum:**
- At least 1 shareholder (can be same person as director)
- At least €1 share capital (can be as low as 1 share at €1)

**Typical Setup:**
- 100 ordinary shares at €1 each = €100 share capital
- Single shareholder (you) owns 100%

**Your Setup (Recommended):**
- **Shares issued:** 1,000 ordinary shares
- **Par value:** €1 per share
- **Share capital:** €1,000 (only €250 must be paid immediately)
- **Ownership:** You own 100% (1,000 shares)

**Why 1,000 shares instead of 1?**
Easy to add co-founders later:
- Want to give someone 10% stake? → Transfer 100 shares (easy math)
- Want to issue options to employees? → Have shares available

**Do you need €1,000 in bank?**
- Only €250 must be paid immediately ("called up")
- Remaining €750 can be paid later ("uncalled capital")
- €250 is deposited in company bank account after company is registered

## Registration Process (Step-by-Step)

### Option 1: DIY via CRO (€50-100)

**Steps:**

1. **Check name availability:** [core.cro.ie](https://core.cro.ie)
2. **Get D-U-N-S number:**
   - Go to [dnb.com/duns-number](https://www.dnb.com/duns-number.html)
   - Request free D-U-N-S for your business
   - Takes 1-2 weeks (do this early!)
   - Free, but required for Apple Developer Organization account later
3. **Prepare documents:**
   - Form A1 (Company Registration Application)
   - Model Constitution (download from CRO)
   - Your ID (passport + proof of Irish address)
4. **Submit online:** [cro.ie](https://www.cro.ie) via CORE portal
5. **Pay fee:** €100
6. **Wait:** 7-10 business days for approval
7. **Receive:** Certificate of Incorporation (PDF + hard copy by mail)

**Difficulty:** Medium (lots of forms, legal jargon)

---

**Recommended Providers:**
1. **Companies.ie** (most popular)
   - Cost: €150 all-inclusive
   - Time: They handle everything, 5-7 business days
   - Includes: Name check, form filing, registered office (1 year)
2. **Duffy & Associates**
   - Cost: €200
   - Similar service to Companies.ie
3. **Formations Ireland**
   - Cost: €180
   - Fast service, good support

**What they do:**

- Prepare all forms (A1, Constitution)
- File with CRO electronically

**Difficulty:** Easy (you fill in online form, they do the rest)

**Recommendation for You:**
Use **Companies.ie** (€150). Worth the €50 extra to avoid CRO form complexity and legal jargon. Time is money.

### Option 3: Accountant/Lawyer (€500-1,000)

## After Company Registration

### 1. Tax Registration (Revenue)

**Within 30 days of incorporation:**

Register for:
- **Corporation Tax** (CT number) - all companies must register
- **PAYE/PRSI** (if you pay yourself salary) - optional initially
- **VAT** (if revenue >€37,500/year) - optional until threshold

**How:**
- Go to [ros.ie](https://www.ros.ie) (Revenue Online Service)
- Register as new business
- Provide: Company name, CRO number, business activity
- Receive: Tax Reference Number (TRN)

**Cost:** Free  
**Time:** Immediate (online registration gives TRN instantly)

### 3. Annual Return (CRO)

**Requirement:**
Every company must file Annual Return with CRO each year.

**What it includes:**
- Financial statements (balance sheet, profit & loss)
- List of directors and shareholders
- Registered office address
- Company secretary details (if appointed)

**Deadline:**
Within 28 days of Annual Return Date (ARD)
- ARD is anniversary of your company registration
- Example: Registered April 15, 2026 → ARD is April 15 every year

**Cost:**
- €28 (if filed on time)
- €100 (if filed late)

**Do you need accountant?**
- Year 1: Maybe not (if no trading activity, very simple return)
- Year 2+: Yes, recommended (€500-1,200/year for small company accounting)

---

### 4. Company Seal (Optional)

**What it is:**
Physical stamp with company name (old tradition).

**Required?**
No, not legally required anymore (since 2015 law change).

**Should you get one?**
Optional. Some banks/lawyers still like it for "official" documents, but digital signatures are now legally valid.

**Cost:** €30-50 if you want one

## Special Considerations for Non-Irish Nationals

### Work Permits and Visas

**If you're in Ireland on work permit:**
- ✅ You can register company (allowed)
- ⚠️ Check if your work permit allows self-employment/business activity
- ⚠️ Working for your own company may require separate permission

**If you're on Stamp 4 (long-term residency):**
- ✅ Full permission to work and run business (no restrictions)

**If you're on student visa:**
- ⚠️ Limited work hours apply (20 hours/week during term)
- ⚠️ Running business may conflict with visa conditions
- 🚨 Check with immigration lawyer before registering company

**Your Situation (Assumption):**
You said you "live in Ireland" without specifying visa type. Assuming you have:
- Work permit with valid employer, OR
- Stamp 4 residency, OR
- EU family member rights

**If any of the above:** ✅ You're fine to register and operate company.

**If unsure about your visa status:** Consult immigration lawyer (€200-300 for 1-hour consultation) BEFORE registering company.

### Tax Residency

**Key Question:** Are you Irish tax resident?

**Irish Tax Residency Rules:**
You're Irish tax resident if:
- You spend 183+ days in Ireland in a tax year, OR
- You spend 280+ days in Ireland over two consecutive tax years

**If you're Irish tax resident:**
✅ Your Irish company's profits are taxed in Ireland only (12.5% corporation tax)
✅ Simple tax structure
✅ No double taxation issues

**If you're NOT Irish tax resident (unlikely if you "live in Ireland"):**
⚠️ May have tax obligations in both Ireland and Nigeria
⚠️ Consult tax advisor

**Assumption for Your Case:**
You live in Ireland → Likely Irish tax resident → Company profits taxed at 12.5% Irish corporation tax (very competitive rate).

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Don't:
1. **Use your Nigerian address** (must be Irish address)
2. **Forget EEA resident director** (you're fine, but if adding non-EEA co-founder, need at least 1 EEA resident)
3. **Skip D-U-N-S number** (you'll need it later for Apple Developer Organization account)
4. **Mix personal/business banking** (open proper business account)
5. **Ignore annual return deadline** (€28 on time, €100 if late)
6. **Forget tax registration** (must register with Revenue within 30 days)
7. **Use company money for personal expenses** (keep clean records)

### ✅ Do:
1. **Check name availability first** (avoid rejections)
2. **Use service like Companies.ie** (save time and stress)
3. **Apply for D-U-N-S early** (takes 2 weeks)
4. **Open Revolut Business** (fast, free, easy)
5. **Keep receipts for everything** (company expenses are tax-deductible)
6. **Set up bookkeeping from day one** (Xero or FreeAgent)
7. **File annual return on time** (save €72 late fee)
