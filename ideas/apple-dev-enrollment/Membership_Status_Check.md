## Apple Developer Program: Individual vs Organization vs Team Member

### 1. Individual Account (What You'd Be Buying)

**Who owns it:** You personally  
**Cost:** €99/year (recurring, NOT one-time)  
**Payment:** Auto-renews yearly unless you cancel  
**Can be revoked?** Yes, if you stop paying or violate terms  

**What you get:**
- ✅ Full control (you're the Account Holder)
- ✅ Upload apps to App Store yourself
- ✅ Manage certificates, provisioning profiles
- ✅ Access to beta software
- ✅ Can invite up to 100 team members (they don't pay)

**Apps show developer as:** Your personal name (e.g., "John Doe")

**Best for:**
- Solo developers
- Personal projects
- Testing/learning
- Non-commercial apps

---

### 2. Organization Account (Company)

**Who owns it:** Registered company (e.g., "Atensai Limited")  
**Cost:** €99/year (same price as Individual!)  
**Payment:** Auto-renews yearly  
**Can be revoked?** Yes, if payment stops or terms violated  

**What you get:**
- ✅ Everything Individual account has
- ✅ Apps show company name (e.g., "Atensai Limited")
- ✅ Can add team members with different roles
- ✅ More professional image
- ✅ Better for commercial apps

**Apps show developer as:** Company name (e.g., "Atensai Limited")

**Requires:**
- Registered company (Irish company in your case)
- D-U-N-S number (free but takes 2 weeks)
- Company email (admin@atensai.com)
- Legal verification by Apple (2-3 days)

**Best for:**
- Commercial apps
- Business/company projects
- Professional branding
- Future investors/exit strategy

---

### 3. Team Member (Added to Someone Else's Account)

**Who owns it:** Someone else (Individual or Organization account holder)  
**Cost:** €0 for you! (Account holder pays €99, can add you for free)  
**Your status:** Guest/Member (not owner)  

**THIS IS LIKELY YOUR SITUATION IF:**
- You can access developer.apple.com and see "Account"
- You didn't pay €99 yourself recently
- You see someone else's company/name as "Account Holder"

**What you get (depends on role):**

#### Role: Admin
- ✅ Almost full control (like owner)
- ✅ Upload apps
- ✅ Manage team members
- ✅ Manage certificates
- ⚠️ Cannot cancel account or change payment

#### Role: App Manager
- ✅ Upload apps
- ✅ Manage app metadata (screenshots, descriptions)
- ✅ Submit for review
- ⚠️ Cannot manage team members or certificates

#### Role: Developer
- ✅ Develop and test apps locally
- ✅ Access certificates and profiles
- ⚠️ Cannot upload to App Store
- ⚠️ Cannot manage team members

#### Role: Marketing
- ✅ Edit app metadata (descriptions, screenshots)
- ❌ Cannot upload builds
- ❌ Cannot access code signing

**Key Limitation:**
If account holder stops paying or removes you, you lose access immediately.

## How Team Membership Works

### Company Adds You as Team Member

**Process:**
1. Company owner enrolls in Apple Developer Program (pays €99/year)
2. Owner goes to "Users and Access" in App Store Connect
3. Owner invites you by email (your Apple ID email)
4. You receive invitation email
5. You accept → You're now team member (free!)

**Benefits for You:**
- ✅ No €99 cost (company pays)
- ✅ Develop and test apps
- ✅ Access beta software
- ✅ May be able to upload apps (if given Admin/App Manager role)

**Risks for You:**
- ⚠️ Company controls your access (can remove you anytime)
- ⚠️ If company doesn't renew, you lose access
- ⚠️ You don't own the apps (company does)
- ⚠️ Cannot use for your personal apps (only company's apps)

## Yearly Fee: How It Works

### Payment Structure

**First Year:**
- Pay €99 when you enroll
- Membership valid for 1 year from enrollment date
- Example: Enroll April 1, 2026 → Expires March 31, 2027

**Renewal (Every Year After):**
- Apple sends email reminder 30 days before expiration
- Auto-renews if payment method on file is valid
- If payment fails, account expires (apps removed from store!)
- You have 90 days grace period to renew before account is closed

**Can You Stop Paying?**
- ✅ Yes, you can cancel auto-renewal anytime
- ⚠️ Apps will be removed from App Store when membership expires
- ⚠️ Cannot upload updates or new apps
- ✅ Can renew later (within 90 days), apps will be reinstated

## FAQ: Membership Confusion

**Q: I paid €99 last year, do I pay again?**  
A: Yes, it's €99/year (recurring). Apple auto-renews if payment method valid. If you didn't renew, membership expired and apps were removed.

**Q: Can I get refund if I'm already enrolled?**  
A: No, Apple Developer membership is non-refundable. Check your status BEFORE paying!

**Q: If I'm team member, can I create my own "sub-account" for free?**  
A: No. Team membership is read/contribute access only. For your own apps, you need separate Individual or Organization enrollment (€99).

**Q: Can two people share one Apple Developer account?**  
A: Technically yes (one pays €99, adds other as Admin), but Apple frowns on this. Each person should ideally have their own account or be properly added as team member.

**Q: How many apps can I upload with one €99 membership?**  
A: Unlimited! Individual accounts can have hundreds of apps. No per-app fee (only annual €99 membership).

**Q: If my company membership expires, what happens to apps?**  
A: Apps removed from store immediately. Users can still use downloaded apps, but can't download again. Renew to restore.

**Q: Can I transfer individual account to organization account later?**  
A: No, you cannot convert Individual to Organization. You'd create new Organization account, then transfer apps using App Transfer feature (complex process).
