## 🆘 FAQ - Quick Answers

**Q: Is Apple Developer €99 one-time or yearly?**  
A: **Yearly.** It auto-renews every year. If you stop paying, apps are removed from store.

**Q: Can I be added to someone's Apple Developer account for free?**  
A: **Yes!** Companies/individuals can add up to 100 team members. You don't pay, but you can't use it for YOUR personal apps.

**Q: Can my company add team members?**  
A: **Yes!** Once you register "Atensai Limited" and enroll as Organization (€99), you can add team members for free (App Managers, Developers, etc.)

**Q: What happens if I'm already enrolled but forgot?**  
A: Check developer.apple.com → If active, use it immediately (don't pay again!). If expired, renew for €99.

**Q: Should I use personal account for dating app?**  
A: **NO!** Dating apps have high liability (lawsuits, data breaches). Use company account for legal protection.

**Q: Can I use team account (where I was added) for my apps?**  
A: **NO!** Ethically and legally wrong. Company owns those apps. You need your own account.

**Q: Do I need Irish citizenship to register Irish company?**  
A: **NO!** Nigerian nationals living in Ireland can register Irish companies. No citizenship required.

**Q: How long does Irish company registration take?**  
A: 7-10 business days with CRO. Use Companies.ie service (€150) to simplify process.
