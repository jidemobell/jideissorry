# Early Filing Decision: Should You Apply Before Late April?

## Quick Decision Tool (2-Minute Read)

**Your Question:** "Should I file Stamp 4 application NOW (March 26) or wait until I'm eligible (late April)?"

---

## The Options

### Option A: File NOW (March 26)
**Time gain:** 5 weeks earlier in queue  
**Risk level:** 🔴 HIGH  
**Recommendation:** ❌ **DON'T DO THIS**

**Why not:**
- 5 weeks before eligibility is too early
- High chance INIS returns application as premature
- If returned, you waste 2-3 weeks + have to resubmit
- Net result: Actually DELAYS your Stamp 4 (worse than waiting!)
- Could flag your file for extra scrutiny

**Only consider if:**
- You have exceptional circumstances (serious hardship)
- Immigration lawyer explicitly says it's okay (get in writing)

**Success rate:** 20-30% (not worth the risk)

---

### Option B: File EARLY (April 14-18)
**Time gain:** 1-2 weeks earlier in queue  
**Risk level:** 🟡 MEDIUM-LOW  
**Recommendation:** ⚠️ **MAYBE** (with lawyer approval)

**Why it could work:**
- Only 1-2 weeks before eligibility (not egregious)
- Given 12-20 week processing delays, you'll be eligible long before they review
- INIS often exercises discretion for applications close to eligibility date
- Include cover letter explaining timing

**Requirements:**
- ✅ Immigration lawyer consultation confirms low risk
- ✅ Include cover letter stating eligibility date
- ✅ All documents perfect (no missing items that could complicate return)

**Success rate:** 70-80% (decent odds if lawyer approves)

---

### Option C: File ON TIME (April 28 or later)
**Time gain:** 0 weeks (but guaranteed acceptance)  
**Risk level:** 🟢 ZERO  
**Recommendation:** ✅ **SAFEST CHOICE**

**Why this works:**
- 100% eligible, zero risk of premature rejection
- No stress, no complications
- INIS cannot question your timing
- Processing time same as early filing (they're backlogged anyway)

**Reality check:**
- Filing 1-2 weeks early only saves you 1-2 weeks in a 12-20 week process
- That's 5-10% time saving for medium-low risk
- Is 1-2 weeks worth potential rejection and restart? Probably not.

**Success rate:** 100% (in terms of acceptance; processing time still 12-20 weeks)
