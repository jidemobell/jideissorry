## Early Filing: Risk vs Benefit Analysis

### The Question:
**Can you file for Stamp 4 BEFORE you're eligible (before late April)?**

### Legal Position:

#### Eligibility Criteria for Stamp 4:
You must have:
1. ✅ Held employment permit for **5 years continuously** (or other qualifying criteria)
2. ✅ Been legally resident in Ireland for qualifying period
3. ✅ Not been out of state for certain periods
4. ✅ No criminal record
5. ✅ Currently on valid immigration permission

**Key word: "held for 5 years"** → Most interpret this as "completed 5 years" not "about to complete."

#### Early Filing Scenarios:

**Scenario A: File 1-2 Weeks Early (mid-April instead of late April)**
- **Risk:** LOW
- **Likelihood of acceptance:** MEDIUM-HIGH
- **Reasoning:** INIS often exercises discretion for applications close to eligibility date
- **Possible outcomes:**
  - Application accepted, processed normally
  - Application held until eligibility date, then processed
  - Application returned as premature (rare if <2 weeks early)

**Scenario B: File 3-4 Weeks Early (early April / first week April)**
- **Risk:** MEDIUM
- **Likelihood of acceptance:** MEDIUM
- **Reasoning:** Clearly before eligibility, but processing delays might mean by the time they review, you're eligible
- **Possible outcomes:**
  - Application held until eligibility date (likely)
  - Application processed if backlog means review happens after eligibility
  - Application returned as premature (moderate risk)

**Scenario C: File Now (March 26, exactly 5 weeks early)**
- **Risk:** HIGH
- **Likelihood of acceptance:** LOW-MEDIUM
- **Reasoning:** Too early, likely flagged as premature
- **Possible outcomes:**
  - Application returned immediately (likely if spotted)
  - Application sits in queue, reviewed after eligibility (possible if backlog severe)
  - Flagged for future scrutiny (risk)

---

### What Immigration Lawyers Say:

**Conservative Advice (most lawyers):**
- "Wait until you're 100% eligible" (late April)
- "Don't risk premature filing, it can delay process further"
- "INIS can refuse application filed early"

**Pragmatic Advice (some lawyers familiar with INIS delays):**
- "1-2 weeks early is often accepted due to processing realities"
- "Include cover letter explaining eligibility date and processing delays"
- "If returned, you lose time but no actual penalty"

**Reality Check:**
- ✅ Many people have filed 1-2 weeks early successfully
- ⚠️ Some applications filed 3-4 weeks early were returned
- 🚨 Filing >1 month early rarely succeeds unless special circumstances

**Action:**
1. **Contact INIS:**
   - Email: info@inis.gov.ie
   - Reference your application number
   - Politely ask for status update
   - Mention processing time exceeds published guidelines

2. **Contact Oireachtas Member (TD):**
   - Find your local TD: whoismytd.com
   - Email them explaining delay
   - TDs can sometimes expedite INIS cases
   - Be polite, explain hardship (business plans affected)

3. **Get lawyer involved:**
   - Immigration lawyer can submit formal query
   - May discover issue with application (missing document)
   - Can sometimes expedite via professional channels

**Don't panic if delayed. INIS delays are normal, frustrating, but eventually resolve.**
