# Atensai.com Website Review & Recommendations

**Last Updated:** March 29, 2026  
**URL Reviewed:** https://www.atensai.com/

---

## What the Site Currently Says (Summary)

The site presents Atensai as a **software agency / consultancy** under the brand "AtensaiLabs" with three service pillars:
- Software Engineering
- Innovation Development
- Educational Research

The primary CTA is "Start a Conversation" and "Discuss Your Project."

---

## The Fundamental Problem

**The website is positioned as a freelance agency. Your business is becoming a product company.**

These are very different things, and the site currently tells the wrong story to every important audience:

| Audience | What They Currently See | What They Should See |
|----------|------------------------|---------------------|
| Potential users of your apps | "A software agency" — not relevant to them | "Products I want to download" |
| Investors / Enterprise Ireland | Generic services page, no traction | Visionary product company with a pipeline |
| Partners / Church networks | Nothing specific | Something directly relevant and trustworthy |
| Potential hires | Generic agency | Exciting startup with a mission |
| App Store users who look you up | "AtensaiLabs" — doesn't match app publisher name | Clear brand identity |

---

## Specific Issues Found

### 1. Brand Name Inconsistency
- Website says **"AtensaiLabs"** and **"AtensaiLabs © 2025"**
- Your intended company name is **"Atensai Limited"** / **"Atensai"**
- The two names create confusion for anyone who looks you up after encountering your apps

### 2. Copyright Date is Stale
- Footer says **© 2025** — it is now 2026
- Small detail but signals the site is not maintained, which undermines credibility

### 3. "Educational Research" Pillar is Disconnected
- Your app portfolio (dating, coordination, finance) has nothing to do with educational research
- This positioning confuses visitors who came from any of your apps
- Unless you intend to pivot into EdTech, this dilutes the message significantly

### 4. Agency Language Throughout
- "Discuss Your Project" → implies clients hiring you
- "Start a Conversation" → passive, agency-style
- "Full-stack development and custom software solutions" → this is subcontractor language
- None of this communicates that you are building *your own products*

### 5. No Product Mention
- The apps you are building (most importantly the dating app) are not mentioned anywhere
- For a product company, the products should be front and centre
- Even a "Coming Soon" teaser creates anticipation and validates the business

### 6. No Geographic Identity
- Nothing about Ireland, EU, or global ambitions
- Given that you are incorporating in Ireland and targeting EU compliance, this is a missed trust signal

### 7. No Personal Story / Mission
- The "Why" is completely absent
- Users and investors want to know who is behind this and what problem you are solving
- A brief founder narrative dramatically increases trust and engagement

### 8. Generic Hero Copy
- "Pioneering software development, educational research, and innovation labs that transform ideas into impactful digital solutions"
- This sentence could describe 10,000 companies and says nothing specific

---

## What the Website Should Do (Strategically)

You are building a **technology product company** based in Ireland, targeting multiple global markets. Your website should:

1. **Establish the brand identity** so that when anyone encounters Atensai (in the App Store, in press, via investors), they arrive at a coherent and credible home
2. **Tease or showcase the products** so that early adopters can register interest
3. **Signal serious intent** to Enterprise Ireland, potential co-founders, and investors
4. **Communicate values** — especially relevant for the Christian app and the dating app (people will want to know if this brand is trustworthy with sensitive personal data)

## Recommended New Structure

### Option A: Product-Forward (Recommended if apps are 3–6 months from launch)

```
HERO
  Atensai — Technology for human connection
  Building apps that bring people together: smarter dating, stronger communities, better finances.
  [Get Early Access] [Learn More]

PRODUCTS
  → [Dating App name] — A different way to meet people (Coming 2026) — [Join Waitlist]
  → [Christian App name] — Community for faith communities in Ireland and beyond (Coming 2026)
  → [Finance App name] — Built for people who think differently about money

ABOUT
  Brief — who is behind this, what problem they are solving, why Ireland is the base

MISSION / VALUES
  3 short paragraphs: honesty, trust, privacy-first, global perspective

CONTACT / SOCIAL
  Simple contact form + social links
```

### Option B: Holding Page (If launch is 6+ months away)

Keep the site minimal and credible — but kill the agency framing entirely:

```
HERO
  Atensai — Building products people love
  [Short description of who you are]

WHAT'S COMING
  [Dating App] — Launching 2026
  [Christian App] — Launching 2026
  [Subscribe for updates]

ABOUT
  One paragraph. Founder background. Based in Ireland. Global vision.

CONTACT
  Email + LinkedIn
```

## Quick Wins (Can Do Today)

These are changes you can make immediately regardless of Stamp 4 / company status:

- [ ] **Fix copyright year** — change "© 2025" to "© 2026"
- [ ] **Change "AtensaiLabs" to "Atensai"** throughout — unify the brand name
- [ ] **Remove or deprioritise "Educational Research"** if it is not part of your plan
- [ ] **Add a coming soon / waitlist section** for the dating app — this costs nothing and starts building an audience now
- [ ] **Add a privacy policy and cookie notice** — even for a landing page, GDPR applies if you're collecting any user data (even email sign-ups)
- [ ] **Add your LinkedIn or X/Twitter** so people can follow you and the company

## Strategic Recommendation on Website vs. Products

> The website right now is an **asset you are not using**. Every week the dating app is in development is a week you could be building a waitlist of interested users. A mailing list of 500 people who signed up before launch is the single most valuable thing you can have on day one.

**The minimum useful version of the website:**  
A single landing page that says what you are building and collects email addresses. That beats the current agency page for all your actual goals.

## Tech Stack Considerations

If you want to update the site yourself:
- If it's currently a static HTML/JS site — easy to modify directly
- If it's on Webflow, Squarespace, Framer, or WordPress — login and edit
- If you want a fast, free rebuild: **Framer** or **Notion + Super.so** are popular for startup landing pages
- For a more serious product-company site: **Next.js + Tailwind** (deployable to Vercel, free tier) — aligns with your existing tech stack
