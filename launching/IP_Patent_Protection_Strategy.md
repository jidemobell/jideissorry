## Overview: Types of IP Protection Available

| Type | What It Protects | Cost | Timeline | Relevance to You |
|------|-----------------|------|----------|-----------------|
| **Patent** | Novel technical inventions | High (€3k–20k+) | 18–36 months | Dating app algorithm (if qualifies) |
| **Copyright** | Code, design, content | Free (automatic) | Immediate | All apps |
| **Trade Mark** | Brand name, logo | Low (€200–600) | 3–6 months | Atensai, app names |
| **Trade Secret** | Confidential processes | Free (NDAs) | Immediate | Algorithm details |
| **Design Right** | Visual appearance (UI) | Low (€100–300) | 2–3 months | App UI |

> **Key Insight:** For a startup, the combination of **Trade Mark + Copyright + Trade Secret** provides 80% of the protection you need at 10% of the cost of patents. Only pursue a patent if the invention is genuinely novel AND you have funds for it.

## The Dating App — Is a Patent Worthwhile?

### What Makes a Patent-Eligible Invention?

For patent protection in Ireland/EU, an invention must be:
1. **Novel** — not previously disclosed anywhere in the world
2. **Inventive step** — not obvious to a person skilled in the field
3. **Industrial application** — capable of being made or used
4. **Technical character** — in EU/Ireland, software must produce a "further technical effect" beyond the software itself (this is the hard part for apps)

### The Challenge with Dating App Patents

Pure "business method" patents (e.g. "a method of matching people based on X criteria") are **not patentable in the EU** under Article 52 of the European Patent Convention. However, if your matching algorithm:

- Uses a novel **technical process** (e.g. novel use of signal processing, computer vision, biometrics)
- Produces a **technical effect** (faster processing, improved accuracy through a novel computational method)
- Is implemented in a non-obvious way at the software architecture level

...then it **may qualify** for a technical patent.

### Questions to Ask About Your Dating App Algorithm

Before spending money on a patent, honestly answer:

1. Is the **matching logic itself** novel, or just the application domain?
2. Does the algorithm solve a **technical problem** (e.g., computational efficiency, a new mathematical model) or just a business/social problem?
3. Could a competitor achieve the same user experience result using a **different technical approach** (meaning they could work around your patent)?
4. Is there anything similar already published in academic papers or existing apps you've reviewed?

> If the answer to Q1 is "the concept/presentation is novel but uses standard ML/algorithm approaches," a patent may be hard to obtain and defend.

### Practical Patent Recommendation for the Dating App

**Phase 1 (Now — free): Document Everything**
- Write detailed internal specifications of the algorithm (date-stamped)
- Store in a private GitHub repo with commit history (this creates evidence of invention date)
- Have a solicitor write and witness a **Statutory Declaration** describing the invention
- This establishes **prior art** in your favour even without a filed patent

**Phase 2 (Pre-launch — ~€500–1,500): Provisional UK/Irish Patent**
- File a **provisional patent application** with the Irish Patents Office (IPOI)
- Cost: ~€30–125 filing fee + solicitor fees (~€500–1,000)
- Gives you **12 months' "patent pending" status** — allows you to test the market
- During this 12 months, decide if a full international patent is worth pursuing
- You can say "Patent Pending" in your marketing — creates deterrence

**Phase 3 (Optional, after revenue — €5,000–20,000): Full Patent**
- **National patent (Ireland):** €1,500–4,000 via a patent attorney
- **European Patent (EPO):** €5,000–15,000 — covers all EU member states + more
- **PCT International:** €8,000–20,000+ — covers 150+ countries including US, UK, Australia
- Only worth doing if you have revenue, investor funding, or a specific licensing strategy

## Trade Secrets Strategy (Immediate, Free)

While you pursue the patent question, **protect the algorithm as a trade secret right now:**

1. **Don't publish details** — don't post algorithm details on GitHub, blog posts, or demo days
2. **Use NDAs** — have anyone who sees your code sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement before reviewing it
3. **Restrict access** — on GitHub, keep algorithm repos private; grant access only on need-to-know
4. **Document everything** — keep a dated engineering log (a private Notion, Obsidian vault, or repo with README entries and commit messages as evidence)
5. **Employee/contractor agreements** — when you hire, include IP assignment clauses (all IP created for the company belongs to the company)

> Trade secrets are practically more powerful than patents for startup algorithms. Companies like Coca-Cola never patented their formula — they kept it as a trade secret. Patents expire (20 years), trade secrets don't.

## Trade Mark Registration — Do This First

**This is the single most important IP action to take early and it's cheap.**

### What to Trade Mark

| Mark | Jurisdiction | Priority |
|------|-------------|----------|
| **ATENSAI** (word mark) | Ireland / EU | High |
| **App name** (dating app) | Ireland / EU | High |
| **App name** (Christian app) | Ireland + EU | Medium |
| **Logo** (if designed) | Ireland / EU | Medium |

### How to Register

**Option 1 — Irish Trade Mark (IPOI only):**
- Covers Ireland only
- Cost: €70 per class (online filing)
- Website: ipoi.gov.ie
- Timeline: 3–5 months

**Option 2 — EU Trade Mark (EUIPO — recommended):**
- Covers all 27 EU member states in one application
- Cost: €850 for 1 class, +50 for each additional
- Website: euipo.europa.eu
- Timeline: 4–6 months
- **Best value for startups targeting Europe**

**Option 3 — International (WIPO Madrid System):**
- Extends an Irish/EU trade mark to 130+ countries
- Cost: Base fee + per-country fees (~$300–600 per country)
- Do this once you have revenue, or if you go global immediately

> **Practical action:** File the EUIPO trade mark for "ATENSAI" in Class 42 (Software/Technology services) and Class 9 (App software) immediately after company incorporation. ~€900 total, done in a day online.

## Copyright (Automatic — No Filing Required)

All code, content, UI designs, and documentation you create are **automatically protected by copyright** at the moment of creation under Irish law (Copyright and Related Rights Act 2000).

**Best practices to strengthen copyright:**
- Add `© 2026 Atensai Limited. All rights reserved.` to your app, website, and code headers
- Keep dated records of creation (commit history, cloud storage timestamps)
- Register copyright with the US Copyright Office (~$65) if you expect US market revenue — US courts require this before filing infringement suits in the US

## IP Ownership: Company vs Personal

**This is critical:** Once you incorporate Atensai Limited:

- Assign all IP you've developed personally to the company via a written **IP Assignment Agreement**
- This is a simple one-page document but must be signed before you raise any investment
- Without this, investors will question who actually owns the app

A solicitor can prepare this for ~€200–500.
