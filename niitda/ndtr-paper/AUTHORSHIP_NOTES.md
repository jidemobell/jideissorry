# Authorship & AI Use — NDTR Paper (ICEGOV 2026)

## The idea is yours. Here is the evidence trail.

### Original Conception
- The Nigerian Digital Travel Registry (NDTR) concept was conceived by **Olajide Adewale Olaniyan**
- Originated during a personal trip to the Philippines in **January 2026**, prompted by firsthand experience with the Philippines eTravel system
- A formal concept note was submitted to the **Director General of NITDA** in January 2026
- NITDA (via Dr. Muhammad Aminu Lawal, SA to DG on Policy) responded on **22 January 2026**, formally acknowledging the proposal and noting it would be reviewed and taken to relevant government counterparts
- A follow-up was sent on **26 January 2026** adding the NIN/financial obligation angle and the asylum fraud dimension

### Primary Research
- The author personally used the Philippines eTravel system (via the eGovPH app) as a Nigerian citizen
  - **Arrival**: Ireland → Philippines, 14 January 2026, cleared at Ninoy Aquino International Airport Terminal 3 (ref: F00IYKX5QMDY)
  - **Departure**: Philippines → Ireland, 20 January 2026 (ref: F00P9CFCUZ7J)
- Screenshots of QR codes, reference numbers, immigration clearance timestamps, and personal profile are saved in this folder

### The Four Policy Pillars (in the author's own words, from the original letter)
1. Counter unfair visa restrictions by demonstrating that the vast majority of Nigerians travel lawfully
2. Enable proactive security monitoring by flagging anomalous travel patterns before incidents occur
3. Strengthen Nigeria's diplomatic position in visa negotiations through evidence-based advocacy
4. Modernise border management in line with global best practices

All four pillars were articulated by the author **before any AI involvement**.

## Compliance with ACM Authorship Policy

ICEGOV uses the ACM ICPS (International Conference Proceedings Series) format.

ACM Policy (updated September 2025) states:
> *"The use of generative AI tools and technologies to create content is **permitted** but must be **fully disclosed** in the Work."*

The Acknowledgements section of the paper contains the required disclosure.

The author meets all three ACM authorship criteria:
- Creator/originator of the idea ✓ (documented January 2026)
- Substantial intellectual contribution ✓ (all four policy pillars, primary research)
- Accountable for all content ✓ (sole listed author)
