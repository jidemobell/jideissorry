# StudyCircle

**Tagline:** Honest struggles. Genuine grace.

## What it is

A Flutter mobile app for Christians to find local Bible study groups and share personal prayer requests anonymously — without fear of judgment.

## The Two Core Problems it Solves

### 1. Finding Real Christian Community Near You
Christians, especially in diaspora or new towns, struggle to find active Bible study groups. Existing tools (Meetup, Facebook Groups) are generic. Church apps are siloed to one congregation. StudyCircle lets anyone discover or create a Bible study group in their town — open to all, not tied to any denomination.

### 2. Anonymous Vulnerability in Christian Community
Christians often cannot share real struggles — addiction, sin, shame — because of fear of judgment from other believers. Yet genuine support exists if people could reach it safely. StudyCircle's prayer/accountability wall lets users share *anonymously to the community*, receive real encouragement, and optionally celebrate milestones — all while keeping the identity wall up for as long as they need.

## The Key Insight
> "Be real without being known."

Most accountability apps require a known partner (Covenant Eyes, Fortify). StudyCircle offers *community-level* anonymous accountability — held by people who care, known to no one.

## Core Features
- **Local group discovery** — find Bible study groups by town/locality
- **Physical or online meetings** — flagged per group
- **One home group** per user — intentional commitment, not social scrolling
- **Guest attendance** — for commuters or people in transition
- **Country-wide anonymous prayer wall** — no location attached, ever
- **Milestone celebration** — "Someone in your community just hit 365 days free" — anonymous, communal, real
- **Optional unveiling** — the person can claim their milestone publicly when ready
- **Prayer stays open** until the owner closes it

## What it is NOT
- A social network
- A dating or friendship app
- A church management tool
- A chat platform (WhatsApp/Signal handle that)

## Target Market
Irish Christians initially, globally scalable. The shame/judgment dynamic is universal across Christian communities worldwide.

## Tech Stack
- **Mobile:** Flutter (iOS + Android from one codebase)
- **Backend:** Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Realtime, Storage)
- **Hosting:** Zero-server, serverless — minimal cost at scale
